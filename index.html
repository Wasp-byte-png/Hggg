A — Location

Where is the Sahara?

In North Africa, about 9.2 million km², nearly the size of the United States. It is bordered by the Atlantic Ocean in the west, the Red Sea in the east, the Mediterranean and Atlas Mountains in the north, and the Sahel in the south.

11 countries are touched by the Sahara
🏔️ Highest point: Emi Koussi (3,415 m) in the Tibesti Mountains, Chad. The Nile crosses it in the east.
